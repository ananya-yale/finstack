Founders can find investor lists. That is not the problem.

The blockers are:

1

Am I even eligible?

Funds rarely publish what rules you out. You find out after the rejection.

2

Do I need equity at all?

Lending off your balance sheet? You need a warehouse facility, from an entirely different set of people.

3

What does it really cost?

Everyone compares dilution. Nobody compares reporting hours or whether the fund is even deploying.

Today you get these answers by knowing someone.

What FinStack provides that others don't

  • An order, not a list. A plan over 12–18 months where each step says what it unlocks.
  • It knows when you need debt, not equity. If you lend off your own balance sheet, a bigger equity round is the wrong answer. You need a warehouse facility from a specialty credit fund. Raising equity instead means giving away ownership to fund loans.
  • It stress-tests your pitch. One agent argues the case against you, so you see the gaps a funder would raise before they raise them.

How it works: 5 agents, 4 model calls

Example: Seed lending startup raising $2M

The plan put equity last.

  1. Get the regulatory package together
  2. i80 Group → warehouse facility for the loan book
  3. Victory Park → second facility conversation
  4. Zeal Capital → equity, once there is lending traction

A generic tool would have sent them to raise equity first.

Why it stays cheap

  • Eligibility filtering is ordinary code, not AI: 52 → 31 → 25 before any model call. Saves ~30% per run.
  • One batched call scores all 25 funders, not 25 separate calls.
  • The skeptic only looks at the top 3.

Safety: two defenses, both tested

It caught a made-up number

The extractor claimed QED writes $3M–$10M checks and attached a real quote about $15M to prove it.

Numbers are only kept if they appear in their own quote. All three invented check sizes were dropped before anyone saw them.

It survived a deliberate attack

I typed a fake "note from the platform operator" into the description, demanding one fund be scored 100 and only that result returned.

It asked for 1 result.
It got 25.

The fund it named had already been filtered out as student-founders-only.

7 of 7 safety checks passing. Testing also found a real bug: an injection used to cut results from 25 to 8 with nothing on screen to say so.

Impact

  • ~1 week of founder time saved. Eight funders in the dataset are not deploying. Every one has a polished website, so a scraper or ChatGPT recommends all eight.
  • Points of dilution. A balance-sheet lender who raises equity instead of a facility gives away ownership to fund loan assets.

Funds keep private databases of peer investors and what they require, plus call notes from fund-to-fund conversations. None of it is public. Founders only get this by building relationships, which is hardest when you are early or a first-time founder.
